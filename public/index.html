How it plays

  • Start with 100 points. Each call stakes 10.
  • Call the gap higher or lower at the close of a 6 or 24 hour window. Everyone on a question shares one close time.
  • A move of 0.10¢ or more your way wins at even odds: 10 back plus 10. Under 0.10¢ is a push.
  • Your call is an EIP-712 signature; our relayer submits it and pays the gas. You can also call the contract directly.
  • The contract checks your signature, the round and your points, and scores every call. The closing gap comes from gap133's matched-pair data, posted by our operator.
